Do's and Don'ts

✓ Do

  • Lead with warmth and encouragement — like a proud, caring mom.
  • Keep one clear decision per screen; the recommendation is the focal point.
  • Use cream surface as the background; white for raised cards.
  • Use numeric tabular figures for all macro numbers.
  • Reserve primary coral for the single top action per screen.
  • Meet WCAG AA on every text pairing.

✗ Don't

  • Don't shame or use error styling to punish a missed goal.
  • Don't overwhelm with dense tables or dashboard-style charts.
  • Don't use hard corners, heavy dark shadows, or stark white backgrounds.
  • Don't use primary-bright for text (decorative only).
  • Don't introduce cool grays or clinical blues as dominant colors.
  • Don't stack multiple coral CTAs competing in one view.
