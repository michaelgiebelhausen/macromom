# MacroMom Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 2, 2026_

MacroMom ("we", "us") is built by BOH Ventures LLC. This policy explains what we
collect, why, and what we never do with it. Host this page at
**https://macromom.app/privacy** (linked from the app's Settings and the App
Store listing).

## What we collect

- **Account info:** your email address (or Apple-provided relay address) and an
  optional first name, so you can sign in and we can say hi properly.
- **Your stats and goals:** sex, age, height, weight, activity level, and your
  goal. We use these for exactly one thing: computing your personal macro
  targets.
- **Dining choices:** which dining halls you follow, the plates we recommend,
  and the meals you choose to log.
- **Plate photos:** photos you take with the portion-check camera. They're
  stored privately (only your account can access them), are stripped of
  location metadata before upload, and are sent to our AI provider only to
  produce your portion feedback.
- **Usage analytics:** anonymous-style product events (e.g. "onboarding
  completed", "recommendation viewed") via PostHog, so we can make the app
  better. No plate photos or health details go to analytics.
- **Crash reports:** error diagnostics via Sentry, scrubbed of tokens and
  personal data.

## What we never do

- We never sell your data. Ever.
- We never share your photos or stats with advertisers.
- We never make medical claims — MacroMom is guidance for everyday eating, not
  medical or clinical advice. Talk to a professional for medical questions.

## Where it lives

Your data is stored with Supabase (hosted Postgres + storage, US region).
Payments run entirely through Apple — we never see your card. Subscription
status syncs to us via RevenueCat using only your account id.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your profile, stats, logs,
subscriptions record, push tokens, and every plate photo. This is immediate and
irreversible. You can also email hello@macromom.app and we'll do it for you.
