## Description

> **MacroMom turns your college dining hall into a personalized meal plan — and
> tells you exactly what to eat today.**
>
> No more guessing at the dining hall. MacroMom reads your hall's real daily
> menu and hands you 2–3 plates that fit your goals — you just grab and go.
>
> **How it works**
> • Tell MacroMom your goal — lose fat, build muscle, maintain, or just eat
>   healthier — and she builds your personal macro targets.
> • Every day she reads your dining hall's actual menu.
> • Before each meal, a warm nudge: here are today's plates, with the macros
>   already done.
> • Snap a photo of your plate for a quick portion gut-check.
> • Watch your week stack up with progress that cheers you on — never guilt.
>
> **Why students love it**
> • *No tedious logging.* MacroMom doesn't make you track — she tells you what
>   to eat.
> • *A real consultant in your pocket.* Built on a certified macro-nutrition
>   consultant's methodology, not internet trends.
> • *It knows YOUR hall.* Real menus from your campus, every single day.
>
> Free week to try. Then less than a couple of coffees a month.
>
> Starting at Clemson. More campuses on the way.
